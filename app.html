About PRC Pakistan

We are a team of certified coaches, counselors, therapists, NLP practitioners, Trauma Healing Therapists and Educators dedicated to strengthening families.

Our Story

Established in 2012, PRC Pakistan began with a simple yet powerful vision: to provide accessible, high-quality parenting and relationship education to families across the nation.

What started as a small initiative by a group of passionate educators and counselors has grown into a trusted organization serving thousands of families. We recognized that many of the challenges parents and couples face in Pakistan — from communication breakdowns to the pressures of modern parenting — require both professional expertise and cultural understanding.

Over the years, we have developed evidence-based programs that honor Pakistani values while incorporating the latest research in family psychology, education, and relationship science. Our mission is to help every family thrive.

🎯

Our Mission

To empower parents, couples, and educators with practical tools and compassionate guidance for building healthier, happier families rooted in Pakistani values.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 August 2026

Our Confidentiality Commitment

What you share with us during coaching, counselling or any session stays with us. Your conversations are treated as private and are never shared without your consent, except where the law requires it or where there is a risk of serious harm. This is the foundation of our work.

Information We Collect

  • • Information you give us — your name, contact details, and the details you share through our contact form, booking requests, feedback, self-assessments or resource feedback.
  • • Information you choose to share in sessions — anything you discuss with our coaches, kept confidential.
  • • Basic usage information — pages visited and similar technical data, if analytics is enabled, used only to improve the website.

How We Use Your Information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for marketing.

  • • To respond to your enquiries and arrange sessions.
  • • To provide coaching, counselling and support services.
  • • To improve our website, articles and resources.
  • • To send you updates only if you have opted in.

Self-Assessments & Scores

Self-assessments in our app run entirely in your browser. Your answers and scores stay on your device and are not sent to us. They are a simple personal check-in, not a diagnosis.

Third-Party Services

Some features rely on trusted third-party services:

  • • WhatsApp — used when you choose to contact us or join our community. Messages are subject to WhatsApp's own privacy terms.
  • • Web3Forms — used to deliver contact and feedback messages to our email.
  • • Canva — used to display our e-books and guides in the resource viewer.
  • • Google Fonts — used for website typography.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us, and we limit access to it within our team. You should also protect any device you use to reach us.

Your Rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, request that it be corrected or deleted, or ask us to stop using it. To make any such request, contact us using the details below.
